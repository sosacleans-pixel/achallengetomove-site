Doing a 5K

Walk to run, then your first 5K, one 12-week level at a time.

Doing a 5K is three 12-week levels. Three quick questions suggest where to start, you pick 3, 4 or 5 days a week, and the app tells you exactly when to walk and when to run. You can repeat any session as often as you like.

The levels

  • Doing a 5K: Walk-to-Run, $4.99. Walk with short jogs, building to 12 minutes of nonstop running.
  • Doing a 5K: 5K, $6.99. Build from 12-minute runs to your first 5K.
  • Doing a 5K: Faster 5K, $6.99. Add speed play, hills and tempo runs for a smoother, quicker 5K.

What’s inside

  • A 12-week program at 3, 4 or 5 days a week, with 14 weeks of access per level (2 extra weeks for missed days)
  • Session timers with voice cues, and a count-up timer for your 5K sessions
  • Streaks, streak saves and badges
  • An optional free 12-minute check before you buy
  • Give any level as a gift
