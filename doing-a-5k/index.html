5K and 10K plans

Walk to your first 5K, get faster, then go 10K. Train 2 to 5 days a week, one level at a time.

Three levels, one at a time. Four quick questions suggest where to start, you pick 2, 3, 4 or 5 days a week, and the app tells you exactly when to walk and when to run. You can repeat any session as often as you like.

The levels

  • A Challenge to Move: Walk to 5K, $6.99. Walk with short jogs, building to your first continuous 5K.
  • A Challenge to Move: 5K Faster, $6.99. Add speed play, hills and tempo runs for a smoother, quicker 5K.
  • A Challenge to Move: 10K, $6.99. For people who can already run a 5K. Build your long run step by step, all the way to 10K.

What’s inside

  • 36 core sessions per level. Train 2, 3, 4 or 5 days a week: 18, 12, 10 or 9 weeks. At 4 or 5 days, the extra days are easy movement or recovery.
  • Your access lasts the length of your plan plus 2 extra weeks. You see the exact end date before you buy.
  • Session timers with voice cues, and count-up timers for your 5K and 10K sessions
  • Streaks, streak saves and badges
  • An optional free 12-minute check before you buy
  • Give any level as a gift

About the 10K level

A Challenge to Move: 10K is general fitness education from Coach Salim Heyaime LLC, not medical advice. It is for healthy adults 18 and older who can already run 5K (3.1 miles) or 30 minutes nonstop. It is not a starting plan for new runners.

Sessions can last up to about 85 minutes, and the 10K check-in can last up to about 103 minutes. Build up gradually, keep your rest and easy days, and don't add extra hard sessions. Walk breaks are always fine.

In heat, slow down, shorten the session, or move it to a cooler time, and drink water before, during and after you go out.

Check with your doctor or a qualified healthcare provider before you start, especially if you are returning after time off, pregnant or postpartum, or have a heart, breathing, joint or other health condition. Stop right away if you feel sharp or worsening discomfort, chest pain or pressure, unusual shortness of breath, dizziness, fainting, or an irregular heartbeat, and get medical help if symptoms are severe or don't settle with rest.
