Doing a 5K: Your Beginner 5K Plan

Go from walking to your first 5K in 8 to 12 weeks.

Doing a 5K takes you from walking to crossing a 5K finish line, about 3.1 miles. You choose how many days a week you can train, and the app sets your plan length to match: about 8 weeks if you train more often, up to 12 weeks if you train less. Each session starts with easy walk-run intervals that build a little at a time, so your body adapts without burning out. The app tells you exactly when to walk and when to run, tracks every session, and keeps you moving with steady encouragement.

What’s inside

  • A plan sized to your schedule: 8 to 12 weeks based on your training days
  • Walk-run interval timers with voice cues
  • Warm-up, cool-down and simple strength moves
  • Progress tracking and a weekly check-in
  • Tips on shoes, pacing and race-day prep
