Gear for Doing a 5K

You don’t need a gym to get race-ready. This short list covers the strength, mobility and recovery work in the Doing a 5K and 5K to Half Marathon plans.

Start with what’s already in your home, then add the essentials and optional extras when you’re ready. Amazon prices are approximate and change often.

Start with what you have

No gear? No problem. These household items cover most of the plan. Buy equipment later, if you want it.

Check that furniture is stable and on a non-slip surface.

Replaces: Step platform and bench

Sturdy chair (no wheels)

Step-ups to a low height, incline push-ups, triceps dips, sit-to-stand squats, or just a hand on the back for balance.

Safety: Push it against a wall so it can’t slide.

Replaces: Step platform

Bottom stair step

Step-ups and calf raises, right where you already are.

Safety: Hold the railing and step with your whole foot.

Replaces: Small dumbbells

Water or milk jugs

A full gallon is about 8.3 lb. Use them for squats, lunges, rows and presses. Pour some out to go lighter.

Safety: Check that the cap is tight and grip the handle firmly.

Replaces: Kettlebell or goblet weight

Backpack filled with books

Hug it to your chest for squats, or hold it by the top handle for deadlifts and rows.

Safety: Keep the straps snug so the load doesn’t swing.

Replaces: Light dumbbells

Canned goods or water bottles

Arm work, shoulder raises and warm-ups.

Safety: Move slowly and keep your wrists straight.

Replaces: Exercise mat

Towel or rug

Lay it down for floor work and put a folded towel under your knees. On hard floors, a towel works as a slider for lunges or mountain climbers.

Safety: Use sliders on smooth floors only, never on carpet or a rug.

Replaces: Support for push-ups and stretches

A wall

Wall sits, wall push-ups and calf stretches.

Safety: Wear shoes or socks with grip so your feet don’t slip.

Replaces: Incline bench

Sturdy table edge or counter

Incline push-ups and hip hinge practice.

Safety: Make sure it won’t tip when you lean on it.

Replaces: Foam roller

Rolled towel or tennis ball

Roll out your feet and calves after a run.

Safety: Go gently and skip any spot that hurts sharply.

Replaces: Form-check bar

Broomstick

Mobility and form drills for hip hinges and overhead reaches.

Safety: Clear the space around you and overhead first.

Replaces: Light resistance band

Old leggings or tights

Tie them into a loop for light band work like side steps and glute bridges.

Safety: Check the knot and fabric for wear before each use.
