The 30-Day Whole Food Plan

Reset how you eat in 30 days, one simple step at a time.

The 30-Day Whole Food Plan helps you get back to real, whole food and discover which foods truly work for your body. You start with a short questionnaire about what you eat, what you avoid, and any sensitivities. From your answers, the app builds your personal food list: what to enjoy, what to pause for 30 days, and what to bring back later.

Each day, the app guides you through one clear step. You log your meals as you go and get instant feedback if something is off-plan, along with a better swap to try. After the 30 days, guided reintroduction helps you bring foods back one at a time and track how you feel, so you finish with a way of eating you can keep.

What’s inside

  • A starting questionnaire and your personal food list
  • A daily step-by-step guide for all 30 days
  • A food log with instant feedback on off-plan choices
  • A plate guide, grocery list and simple meal ideas
  • A reintroduction tracker to learn what your body likes
