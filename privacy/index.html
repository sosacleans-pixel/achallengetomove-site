Privacy

This website, achallengetomove.com, and the companion apps on it are operated by Coach Salim Heyaime LLC (Salt Lake County, Utah). This notice explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Notify-me sign-up

When you sign up to be notified about new challenges, the email address you enter is emailed to Coach Salim through the email-forwarding service FormSubmit. We use it only to tell you when new challenges and programs launch. We do not sell it.

Purchases

App purchases are handled by Stripe on Stripe's own checkout page. Stripe collects your payment details under its own privacy policy; we never see or store your full card number. Paperback books are sold by Amazon, which handles that order under its own policy.

Companion apps

The challenge apps hosted on this site save your progress (such as checked-off days, settings and any photos you add) in your own browser on your device. That data is not sent to us. Clearing your browser data removes it.

The A Challenge to Move app at app.coachsalimheyaime.com uses email sign-in. The email address you sign in with is used only to give you access to the app.

Analytics and fonts

This site uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use it (pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser type). Google Analytics sets cookies and processes this data under Google's privacy policy; you can block these cookies in your browser settings or with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on. We also record anonymous events when you click a link to Amazon or Stripe checkout, or sign up to be notified (which link, or that the form was sent); these never include your email address. We do not use advertising cookies. Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives standard technical information (such as your IP address) when a page loads.

Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

This section is our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy. It applies to residents of Washington, Nevada and other states with similar consumer health data laws, and it covers achallengetomove.com and the challenge apps hosted on it, including A Challenge to Move: 30 Days Before X-mas Challenge. These services are provided by Coach Salim Heyaime LLC.

What we collect

When you buy a challenge, or buy one as a gift, we receive your name, your email address and your purchase details. For a gift, we also receive the recipient's email address and the gift details. Payment is processed by Stripe on its own checkout page; we never see or store your full card number.

Why we collect it

We use this information only to deliver your purchase and your access (or your gift recipient's access), to send receipts and service messages, to handle refunds and support, and to keep the business and tax records the law requires.

Never sold, never used for advertising

We never sell your personal information or consumer health data, and we never use it, or share it, for advertising.

Your workout and health inputs stay on your device

Everything you enter in a challenge app, such as readiness answers, check-offs, test results, effort and pain notes, habits, steps you type and check-in notes, is saved only in your own browser on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and we can't see it. The challenge apps have no advertising pixels, no analytics and no location tracking or geofencing.

Your right to access and delete

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, and ask us to delete it, by emailing coachsalimheyaime@gmail.com. We may keep records the law requires us to keep, such as tax records. Data stored only on your device can be deleted from the app (or by clearing this site's data in your browser), because we don't have a copy of it.

Marketing email

We send marketing email only if you opt in with a separate checkbox that is never checked for you. Buying a challenge or receiving a gift does not add you to our marketing list. Every marketing email has an unsubscribe link.

Questions

For any privacy question, or to ask us to delete your email address, contact coachsalimheyaime@gmail.com.
