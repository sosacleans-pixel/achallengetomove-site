Privacy

Effective date: [EFFECTIVE DATE].

This website, achallengetomove.com, and the companion apps on it are operated by Coach Salim Heyaime LLC (Salt Lake County, Utah). This notice explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Notify-me sign-up

When you sign up to be notified about new challenges, the email address you enter is emailed to Coach Salim through the email-forwarding service FormSubmit. We use it only to tell you when new challenges and programs launch and, only if you tick the separate box on the form, to send you news and offers. We keep a record of what you agreed to and when. We do not sell it.

Purchases

App purchases are handled by Stripe on Stripe's own checkout page. Stripe collects your payment details under its own privacy policy; we never see or store your full card number. Paperback books are sold by Amazon, which handles that order under its own policy.

When you buy an app, we receive your name, email address and purchase details from Stripe. We use your email address to send receipts and service messages about your purchase and your access (such as sign-in links, your access end date and notices about changes to your access), and to handle refunds and support. Buying an app does not add you to a marketing list.

Companion apps

The challenge apps hosted on this site save your progress (such as checked-off days, settings and any photos you add) in your own browser on your device. That data is not sent to us. Clearing your browser data removes it.

The 22-Day 7-Minute Workout app at app.coachsalimheyaime.com uses email sign-in. It saves your workout days, streaks and reminder settings to your account on our database provider's servers and, only if you agree, your energy and mood ratings and written reflections. It has its own Privacy Policy and Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy, which explain what it collects and your choices.

Analytics, cookies and your choices

Analytics, cookies and your choices. We use Google Analytics on achallengetomove.com (and on the signed-out welcome screen of app.coachsalimheyaime.com) only if you choose "Accept analytics." With your consent, it records the pages you view (which can include pages about specific fitness programs), clicks on links to Amazon or to checkout, your approximate location (city level, from your IP address) and your device and browser type. We have turned off Google signals and advertising features, and Google processes this data for us as our service provider. We never send names, email addresses, purchase details or anything you enter in an app to Google Analytics. Our challenge apps never load Google Analytics. You can change your choice at any time with "Cookie settings" at the bottom of every page.

Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control (GPC) or Do Not Track signal, we treat it as a refusal of analytics and as a request to opt out of any sale, sharing or targeted advertising, and we don't load Google Analytics. We do not sell personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or use it for targeted advertising.

Other sites. Other than Google Analytics when you accept it, we don't allow third parties to collect information about your online activity over time and across other websites through our sites. Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address.

Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy (for residents of Washington, Nevada, Connecticut and similar states) is on its own page: Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy.

Where your information is stored

Where your information is stored. We're based in the United States, and our service providers (Supabase, Resend, Stripe, Google and GitHub) store and process your information in the United States and, for some providers, other countries where they operate. If you live outside the US, including in Canada or Quebec, your information is transferred to the US. There it is protected by our agreements with these providers, but it may be accessible to US courts, law enforcement and national security authorities under US law. Before transferring personal information of Quebec residents outside Quebec, we assess the privacy risks and require written protections from the recipient. Questions? Contact our person in charge of privacy at coachsalimheyaime@gmail.com.

Your privacy rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • confirm whether we have personal information about you and give you a copy, in a portable, machine-readable format if you ask (in the app: Download my data);
  • correct information that is inaccurate;
  • delete your information (in the app: Delete my account and data). We also tell our service providers to delete it. We keep only records the law requires, such as tax and payment records;
  • withdraw any consent you gave, for example to saving check-ins or to marketing email;
  • opt out of the sale of your personal information, targeted advertising, and profiling that leads to decisions with legal or similarly significant effects. We do none of these, but you can still ask, and we'll confirm in writing.

We won't charge you, refuse service or treat you differently for using these rights.

How to ask. Use the tools in the app, or email coachsalimheyaime@gmail.com with "Privacy request" in the subject line from the email address you use with us. We'll confirm the request comes from you, for example by asking you to reply from that address or sign in. Someone you authorize can ask for you with your signed permission; we may still confirm your identity with you. We respond within 45 days (within 30 days for residents of Canada). If we need more time, we'll tell you why within that period: up to 45 more days in the US, or as Canadian law allows.

Appeals (United States). If we deny your request in whole or in part, reply to our answer with "Appeal" in the subject line. We'll answer in writing within 45 days and explain why. If you're not satisfied, you can contact your state attorney general, for example: Washington (atg.wa.gov/file-complaint), Nevada (ag.nv.gov), Connecticut (portal.ct.gov/ag), California (oag.ca.gov/contact/consumer-complaint-against-business-or-company) or Colorado (coag.gov).

Consumer health data. If you live in Washington, Nevada, Connecticut or another state with a consumer health data law, our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy describes the extra rights you have, including a list of everyone we've shared your health data with.

California. We don't share personal information with third parties for their own direct marketing. You can review and correct your information as described above. We explain how we respond to Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals in "Analytics, cookies and your choices."

Canada. If you live in Canada, you have rights under the federal Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and, where they apply, the private-sector privacy laws of Alberta, British Columbia and Quebec. You can access and correct your information and withdraw consent (subject to legal or contractual limits; we'll tell you what withdrawing means for your use of the app). You can also complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (priv.gc.ca), the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec (cai.gouv.qc.ca), the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner of Alberta (oipc.ab.ca) or of British Columbia (oipc.bc.ca). Please contact us first so we can try to help.

Quebec. You can also: ask how we got your information and who can see it; be told about, and turn off, any technology that can identify, locate or profile you (our only such technology is optional Google Analytics on our website, which is off unless you accept it); get your information in a structured, commonly used technological format; and ask us to stop disseminating your information or to de-index links to it where the law allows. We don't make decisions about you based only on automated processing.

How we handle privacy
