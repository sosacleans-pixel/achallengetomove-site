Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Effective date: [PUBLISH DATE]. This is our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy. It applies to residents of Washington, Nevada and other states with similar consumer health data laws, and to anyone whose consumer health data is collected in Washington or Nevada (Washington's My Health My Data Act, RCW 19.373, and Nevada's NRS 603A.400 to 603A.550). It covers achallengetomove.com and the challenge apps hosted on it, including A Challenge to Move: 30 Days Before X-mas Challenge, Doing a 5K and the 30-Day Whole Food Plan. These services are provided by Coach Salim Heyaime LLC, a Utah limited liability company in Salt Lake County, Utah ("we", "us").

Because our challenges are fitness and wellness programs, the fact that you bought one, received one as a gift or asked us about one could show that you are seeking to improve your health. We treat that information as consumer health data.

Consumer health data we collect, and how we use it

  • Purchase and gift details. When you buy a challenge, or buy one as a gift, we receive your name, your email address and your purchase details (what you bought, when, and the amount). For a gift, you give us only your own email address, and we email the gift code to you. We never collect the recipient's email address from you. The recipient gives us their own email address when they redeem the code. We also keep the gift details, including whether the gift code has been redeemed. We use this to deliver your purchase and your access (or your gift recipient's access), to send receipts and service messages, to handle refunds and support, and to keep the business and tax records the law requires. Payment is processed by Stripe on its own checkout page; we never see or store your full card number.
  • Messages you send us. If you email us (for example, to ask for a refund or for help), we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. We use it only to answer you and handle your request.
  • Notify-me and marketing email. If you sign up to be notified about new challenges, we receive your email address. Notify-me signups join our marketing list only if you tick the opt-in box. At checkout, we send marketing email only if you tick a separate checkbox that is never checked for you. Buying a challenge or receiving a gift does not add you to our marketing list. Every marketing email has an unsubscribe link.
  • Website usage. Our website (not the challenge apps) uses Google Analytics, which records the pages you view (which can include pages about specific fitness programs), clicks on links to Amazon, approximate location derived from your IP address, and your device and browser type. GA records that a checkout button was clicked, but not which product. We use it to understand how the website is used. Like any website, our web host also receives standard technical information, such as your IP address and browser type, when a page or app file loads.

Your workout and health inputs stay on your device

Everything you enter in a challenge app, such as readiness answers (including health conditions or pregnancy), check-offs, test results, effort and pain notes, habits, steps you type, check-in notes and any photos you add, is saved only in your own browser on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and we can't see it, so we don't collect it. The 30 Days Before X-mas Challenge, Doing a 5K and the 30-Day Whole Food Plan use email sign-in (Doing a 5K and the 30-Day Whole Food Plan starting [GATING DATE]): you sign in with a one-time link sent to your email. To give you access, we keep your email address and your access dates. Your challenge progress stays on your device, and so do the health inputs listed above. None of our challenge apps have advertising pixels, analytics or location tracking or geofencing, and none of them sync with wearables or health apps.

Where the data comes from

  • From you, when you check out, fill in a form on our website or email us.
  • From Stripe, which confirms your payment and sends us your name, email address and purchase details.
  • From a gift recipient, who gives us their own email address when they redeem a gift code. We never collect a recipient's email address from the buyer.
  • Automatically from your browser, through Google Analytics cookies and our web host's standard logs.

Who we share it with

We share consumer health data only with the service providers below, and only so they can provide their service to us:

  • Stripe (payment processing): your name, email address, payment and purchase details.
  • Supabase (access records): your email address, your purchase details (what you bought, when, and the amount), your access dates and, for a gift, whether the gift code has been redeemed.
  • Resend (email): your email address and the service emails we send you, such as sign-in links, purchase confirmations with your access end date, gift codes, access reminders and notices about changes to your access.
  • Google (our email provider, Gmail; Google Analytics on our website; and Google Fonts on our website pages): emails you send us and that we send you, Notify-me sign-ups forwarded to us by email, the website usage data described above, and standard technical information when website pages load fonts.
  • FormSubmit (the service that forwards Notify-me sign-ups to our email): your email address.
  • GitHub (our web host): standard technical information, such as your IP address and browser type.

When you buy a gift, we email the gift code to you, the buyer, not to the recipient. We may disclose information if the law requires it, for example in response to a valid court order. We don't share consumer health data with any affiliate.

Never sold, never used for advertising

We never sell your personal information or consumer health data, and we never use it, or share it, for advertising.

Tracking over time and across websites

Google Analytics sets cookies on our website that let Google recognize your browser across visits to this website over time. Google may also collect information about you on other websites and apps that use Google services, under Google's privacy policy. You can block these cookies in your browser settings or with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on. The challenge apps don't load Google Analytics or any other tracker.

Your rights and how to use them

You can ask us to:

  • confirm whether we collect, share or sell consumer health data about you;
  • give you access to that data, together with a list of every third party and affiliate we have shared it with or sold it to;
  • stop collecting or sharing it, and withdraw any consent you gave us;
  • delete it. We will also tell the service providers we shared it with to delete it, except for records that we or they must keep by law, such as tax and payment records;
  • correct your name or email address.

To make a request, email coachsalimheyaime@gmail.com with "Consumer health data request" in the subject line, from (or including) the email address you used with us. To protect your data, we may ask you to confirm details only you would know before we act on the request. We will respond within 45 days. If we need more time, we will tell you within those 45 days and may take up to 45 more days. Requests are free (in Washington, up to twice in any 12-month period), and we won't treat you differently for making one.

Data stored only on your device can be deleted from the app (or by clearing this site's data in your browser), because we don't have a copy of it.

Appeals. If we decline your request, in whole or in part, you can appeal by replying to our answer with "Appeal" in the subject line. We will answer your appeal in writing within 45 days and explain our decision. If we deny your appeal, you can contact the Washington Attorney General at atg.wa.gov/file-complaint or the Nevada Attorney General at ag.nv.gov.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here with a new effective date. Before we collect new categories of consumer health data, use it for new purposes or share it with new recipients, we will tell you and ask for your consent first.
