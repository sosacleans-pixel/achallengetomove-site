What is A Challenge to Move?

Move better. Eat better. Feel better.

App-guided challenges from Coach Salim Heyaime, from a 7-minute daily workout to your first 5K and beyond. Pick a challenge, follow the daily plan, and build habits you’ll keep.

A Challenge to Move is a wellness hub with tools to help you move better, eat better and feel better, one small challenge at a time. Coach Salim Heyaime has coached online clients around the world since 2017, and he designs each program to fit real, busy lives. Start with a 7-minute daily workout, then level up to a whole-food plan, a first 5K, a half marathon, or a 75-day challenge. Each app gives you the day’s plan, timers and a check-off to keep you going.

About

Coach Salim Heyaime has coached online clients around the world since 2017. He created A Challenge to Move to make wellness approachable: a few focused minutes a day, not hours at the gym.

These programs pair practical books with apps that coach you through each day. Learn more at coachsalimheyaime.com.
