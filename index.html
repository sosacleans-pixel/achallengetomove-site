What is A Challenge to Move?

Move better. Eat better. Feel better.

A Challenge to Move is a wellness hub from Coach Salim Heyaime: guided apps, eating plans and a book that turn one small daily challenge into a habit you keep.

Start with 7 minutes a day. Walk to your first 5K. Reset how you eat. Go 75 days. Pick a challenge, follow the day’s plan, check it off, repeat.
