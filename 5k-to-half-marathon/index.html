5K to Half Marathon: Your First 13.1 Plan

Build from 5K to 13.1 miles in 12 or 16 weeks.

For runners who can already run a 5K and want a plan to build up to a first half marathon (13.1 miles, 21.1 km). A short run check sets your plan: 16 weeks if you can run 20 to 39 minutes without stopping (or 30 minutes of run-walk), 12 weeks if you already run 40 minutes or more about three days a week. You pick 3 or 4 run days a week. The app guides every session with a timer, adds two short strength sessions a week, and tapers you into race day.

What’s inside

  • A plan set by your run level and schedule: 12 or 16 weeks, 3 or 4 run days a week
  • Easy runs, workouts and a weekly long run, with an effort guide (RPE and talk test)
  • Two short strength sessions a week, set by a push-up check, with or without equipment
  • Run and strength timers with voice cues and check-offs
  • Works offline once opened; app screens in English or Spanish (detailed coach notes in English)
